We are interested in:
  • Artificial Intelligence and Deep Learning
  • Protein and RNA Structure Prediction
  • De Novo Protein and Peptide Design
  • Structure-Based Protein Function Annotation
  • SNP Mutation and Genetic Diseases
  • Protein-Protein and Protein-RNA Interactions
  • Ligand Docking and AI-Driven Drug Design
Understanding the structure and function of proteins and RNAs is fundamental to modern biology and medicine. Our lab develops advanced AI-driven algorithms to predict the three-dimensional structures of proteins and RNAs directly from their primary sequences, and to elucidate their biological functions through an integrated sequence–structure–function framework. We further combine large-scale computational modeling with biochemical and biophysical validation to design novel biomolecules—including proteins, peptides, and RNAs—with enhanced or entirely new functionalities beyond those found in nature.
